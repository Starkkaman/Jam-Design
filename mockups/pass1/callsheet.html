Jam Design · Overnight pass 1 · Callsheet

Callsheet, less daunting

A redesign that starts from what you need when you open it. You get one sentence about the day, the day on a tuning scale, and three stops: replies, what's worth knowing, and what's coming. Everything else is one step away. The one orange button moves to whatever is next. All names, mail and events are made-up sample data.

2 · The first screen

Both frames work. Click the desktop frame and try Enter (start replies), A (agenda), / or ⌘K / Ctrl+K (find or ask), 1–4 (tabs), and in replies C copy, D done, L later, X not for me, Esc back. On the phone, swipe a reply card right for done and left for later.

3 · One step away

The follow-on states, each frame live. Copying a draft never sends anything; Done and Later always offer Undo.

What's different from Callsheet 0.9.0

One sentence, three stopsToday drops from about a dozen sections (Start your day, summary, Weekly review, Top of the day, Security, By area, What changed, Coming up, At a glance) to a sentence, the day scale and three stops. The rest is one quiet line or ⌘K away.
The button that movesThere is one orange button on every screen. On Today it sits on the next thing: Start replies in the morning, See tomorrow once replies are clear. The Replies stop's count bars go dark one by one as you work.
Fun from the hardwareThe day is a radio tuning scale with a needle at now. Clashes light amber, not red, because red is kept for security alerts. All clear gets the Session blocks accent, dropped in once.
