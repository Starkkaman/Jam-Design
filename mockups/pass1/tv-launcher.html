What's on it, and where it came from

Top shelf (Apple tvOS)The focused item fills the top of the screen with its title and one line. On a cover it's what you were watching. On an app it says what the app is for. No autoplaying previews.
Inputs and settings up top (Google TV)The top bar holds Inputs, Quick settings and Settings, one press up from anywhere. Quick settings slides in from the right like tvOS's Control Center: picture, sound, sound out, sleep timer.
Only your stuff (Projectivy)Rows are Continue, Apps (you choose and order them) and Inputs. There's no "For you", no sponsored row, and nothing you didn't put there. All apps is a plain grid. Android settings is one entry away.
