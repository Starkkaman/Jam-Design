JamFin in Braun Hi-Fi: Home, a film, the title card and the player

The core TV journey for the Jellyfin client on the living-room Hisense: Home opens on your first Continue watching card, OK opens the film page on Resume, OK plays. The player menus pop above (chapters) or below (settings) the timeline, as in Unigrid. Click the TV and use the arrow keys, Enter (OK) and Esc (Back), or the remote buttons. What's on it, and why

Progress and selector, splitResume progress stays on the art's bottom edge (a JamFin fixed zone). The selector window moves to the bottom of the label plinth, half the width and a title's height away. Flip "Progress vs selector" to see the pass-1 problem: two cream bars a few pixels apart.
Menus pop above or belowIn the player, ↑ opens chapter cards above the band and ↓ lifts the band onto a settings strip. A list from the strip rises above the band and never covers the timeline. On the film page, Audio and Subtitles open the same kind of list beside the action.
One orange per screenHome has none (the selector carries focus). On the film page it's Resume. In the player it's none. On the phone it's Resume on the card you've scrolled to, and it moves with you.
Said onceThe card's bar shows how far you are. The sign above says when it would end. The playhead carries its own time, so there's no second "1:12:04" on the band.
Per-profile lookAda's profile keeps Braun Hi-Fi, Night and Full colour covers. "Toned" pulls cover colour down for profiles that want a calmer wall. The house rule allows this only in JamFin.
Title cardPressing Resume closes the flat ribbed shutter with the title on the seam. The slatted bar shows the real stream load, and the shutter opens on the player when it's ready. In Calm it becomes a short fade.
