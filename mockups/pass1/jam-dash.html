Jam Design · Overnight pass 1 · Jam-Dash

Jam-Dash, trouble first

Jam-Dash in the simplified Braun Hi-Fi look. Home shows both servers on one screen. Anything that needs you comes first, as a short row of trouble cards, and the one orange button sits on the first fix. Under that, each server gets a black faceplate with processor, memory and disk, then every running app as a tile with an Open key. Navigation is never hidden: all seven places sit in the top bar on desktop. The apps, numbers and log lines are made-up sample data. The host names are the README's "home-server" and "nas".

2 · Home, both servers

Click a tile to open its detail. Click a tile's ↗ key to "open" the app. Restart Crafty runs the job: the card's window blinks while it restarts, the card leaves, and the orange button moves on to the next fix. With everything up, the Stripes accent comes back. On desktop, Enter presses the orange button, 1–7 switch places, and Esc goes back.

3 · App detail

Crafty, which is unhealthy. The page reads top to bottom: what's wrong, the fix, why (failing log lines carry a ▲ as well as the colour), then resources, addresses and ports, and facts. On a healthy app, Open becomes the orange button.

4 · Store (optional this pass)

The Store opens on updates, because updating is the weekly job and browsing isn't. The catalog follows, with search and every category visible as keys. Get opens the install sheet, where the checks (port, folder, disk, processor) run before you commit.

5 · Rules this mockup follows

Container states → shared wordsRunning = on (green round LED). Stopped by you = off (dark window). Server unreachable = offline (dashed outline, no face). Starting, restarting or updating = action (cream, blinks 3 times, then holds). Unhealthy, or stopped when it shouldn't be = alert (red LED, red ring, and the word). Disk over 90% = warn (amber). The tiles show readable words, such as "Running · 12 d", with the shared state on data-state.
Orange, onceOne orange button per screen, and it sits on the next thing to do: the first fix on Home, Restart on a broken app, Open on a healthy one, and Update all in the Store. When a job finishes, it moves on, the same "moving primary" as Callsheet. Stop is dark ink and always asks first.
Motion on change onlyMeters fill once when you arrive. Cards slide into place when one leaves. A job fills a cream slot in its button. New log lines rise in. Nothing moves on a metrics tick. Calm (or reduced motion) cuts all of it to short fades. When there's trouble, the accent steps back.
