Title card

The card that covers the gap between pressing Play and the film or game starting. It's also the place to hide loading: each card shows real progress and only hands off when the thing is ready. Pick a style and a load speed, then press Play, or click the TV and press Enter on a tile.

The three styles

ShutterThe two halves of the band slide in from either side and close over the screen. The title sits on the seam, and the lit strip under it is the load. When it's full, the halves slide away to reveal the film. Closest to Round 5's card.
Tape counterThe screen becomes the faceplate of a tape deck. Title on the left. On the right, a seven-segment counter runs 000 to 100 as it loads, with a punch label that reads LOADING, then READY as its light comes on. Calmest of the three.
Tuning dialA backlit radio dial warms up, and a red needle sweeps across as the thing loads, like tuning into a station. "Tuned" lights when it's ready. The most character, and it reads from the sofa.

All three stay dark: the only bright things are small lit windows, so nothing flashes in a dark room. They animate only movement and fades, which the TV boxes handle well. A fast load still holds the card for about 1.6 seconds so the title can be read; a slow load holds it until loading actually finishes.
