Signature: light reveal

The rule: when something becomes active, its orange light is revealed. Nothing flashes on. A shutter slides back or a glow warms up. It's one idea used everywhere, so the whole house feels like one piece of equipment.

What lights upOnly things that are active or in progress: what's playing, what's on, what's focused on the TV, what's loading. Idle controls stay unlit.
How it movesThe shutter opens in about 0.6–1.4 s on the settle curve. Glows warm in over about 0.4 s and cool off faster. Focus itself stays fast (150 ms); the light grows after focus lands.
Why it's cheapThe shutter moves with a transform and the glow fades with opacity: the two things your TV hardware handles well. No blur, and nothing flashes.
