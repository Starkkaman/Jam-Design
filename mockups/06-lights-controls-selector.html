Controls and indicators

Indicators light upStatus lights, meters and progress show state. They are the only things that glow.
Controls don't glowSwitches and buttons use colour (the orange paddle) and position, never light. The one exception: a slider reveals light up to where you set it, because the light is its reading.
Straight controls onlyNo knobs, dials or curved controls you have to drag. Arcs and dials are read-only.
Meters are read-onlyThe quiet face, lit scale and ladder show levels. To change a level you use a straight slider next to it.
