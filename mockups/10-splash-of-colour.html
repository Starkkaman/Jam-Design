Jam Design · Hi-Fi · Colour studies

Splash of colour

Nine ways to add a splash of colour to the Hi-Fi screens, each drawn from your influences. All are original shapes: no logos, emblems or copied designs. Every one stays in the clear zone (top right), so nothing sits behind a title or a button. They're flat, static shapes, which are cheap on the TV. The decorative oranges are kept darker than the button orange, so they never read as something to press.

They can also mix

One splash per screen, but different screens (or apps) could each get their own. For example: Stripes on the TV launchers, Mission patch on the NervOS boot, Braun keys on Jam-Dash, Horizon on the Jam Home screensaver.
