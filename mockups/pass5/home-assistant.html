Jam Home, pass 5: the house, honestly drawn

Pass 4 drew Jam Home as a house: a lit plan, a day arc, wall plates, a sideboard, keys on a rail. Pass 5 keeps all of it and fixes what didn't hold up. The plan is now a labelled placeholder with a "Draw the house" setup flow, because a plan of the wrong house is worse than none. The lamps and the door are a small set of drawings other apps can reuse (Jam Home owns them). Lamps no longer go brown at night, and the door is amber by day and red after dark. Everything is sample data (Tue 2:30 pm for Day, 9:40 pm for Night). Tap anything. Nothing here changes Home Assistant.

4 · New screen

Draw the house (Settings › Plan)

The plan is a claim about the real house (H17), so it is drawn by the people who live there and labelled as theirs. It starts from the sample. Pick a room, tap two opposite corners on the grid, and set each door and window on a wall. Saving updates every plan on this page to "Drawn by Isaac". The squares are 44 px, the touch floor, which is why the grid is 8 × 7.

How it works

1 Rooms. Areas with lamps come from Home Assistant and are always on the plan. Rooms without lamps (Bath, Hall, Entry) are optional; they make the plan readable. A lamp can be marked as outside the walls (the Car port is in HA's Kitchen area).

2 Place. An 8 × 7 grid. The top row is for things outside the walls. Overlaps are shown hatched and named, and Next still works (the last one placed wins).

3 Doors and windows. Only openings HA can see: a lock or a sensor. Each goes on one wall of one room, near its start, middle or end. A door always opens into its room.

4 Check. The plan with the house's lights as they are now. Save plan is the one orange key. The end screen has none.

Capacity (H16): up to 10 zones on 8 × 7 squares. A house that needs more draws the busiest floor and lists the rest as tiles under it.

5 · House objects · conflict 9

The lamps and the door, drawn once

Jam Home owns the drawings of the house's own things. Other apps (Lobby's house plate, anything that shows a lamp or the door) reuse these and don't redraw them. Each is an object-shaped window (H3): an unlit recess when off, a flat fill when on, never growing or glowing. Each scales by its viewBox (30 units), so strokes stay 1.5 / 2 / 3.2 px at 30 / 40 / 64 px.

TV variantOn a dark TV ground an unlit shade gets a 1.6 px grey rim, so the window stays visible (IN2). Strokes turn warm grey and stay ≥ 3 px at 1080p. Labels are 26 px or more on the TV itself (shown here at 13 px).
TV focusThe object lifts 6 px (transform only), and a cream sill slot under it lights, at most 150 ms, colour snapping. The shade keeps showing the lamp's own state, so focus never looks like "on" (TV1). Lobby supplies the plate around it.
Rules for reuseLamp colour always goes through the display colour and the orange guard. The door is amber (warn) or red (alert) by the conflict-12 rule. The hinge side comes from the drawn plan. Never recolour, outline or animate these, apart from the focus lift.
