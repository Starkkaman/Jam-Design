Jam-Dash, pass 5: the whole rack

Jam-Dash runs Docker apps on two servers, "home-server" and "nas". Pass 4 drew it as equipment: each server a rack, each app a module, a board of lights for the glance, tags for trouble, cords for what needs what, and a tape of the last 24 hours. Pass 5 keeps that idea, fixes the pass-4 audit, and carries the rack into every other screen: the Store, the full log, Edit setup, Files, Shares, first run and History. All names, numbers and log lines are made-up sample data.

What changed since pass 4

  • Cords say where they come from. A solid cord is found in real data: a mount, a compose link, or a proxy host read through Proxy Manager's connection. A hollow cord was drawn by you. A guess is never drawn: it waits on the app's cord panel as a question ("Does Sonarr need Jellyfin?").
  • Cords stay tidy at scale. They run in combed lanes down the cord channel (renamed from "patch field"), so many cords make one bundle, not spaghetti. Cords to folded apps end on the plate with a count, and the note above the racks names them.
  • A busy day folds. When a rack is taller than the screen, healthy apps fold into a Running plate ("Running · 25"). Trouble, live previews, jobs and apps you keep in view stay out. The plate opens into a shelf of half-width modules.
  • Day is light. The cabinet is lab-grey enamel by day. Only windows that hold words or numbers stay dark (the board's legends, the head unit's display). The tape is a light strip by day and the black ribbed band at night.
  • The tape zooms. "24 h | Last hour". At 24 h, marks closer than 24 px merge into one mark with a count, and pressing it opens the last hour.
  • The phone gets the rack sooner. The phone board turns sideways: servers are rows and the five subjects are columns. The rack picker moved to the rack itself. In Trouble the rack now starts on the first screen.
  • 1280 fits without emergency tightening: the rack folds (Auto) and the tag rail uses its ladder.
  • New screens on the rack: Store and install (a catalogue drawer and the bench), the full log (with the app's tape lane as a scrubber), Edit setup (the module's rear panel: ports, folders, environment, secrets, cords), Files (places as drive bays), Shares, first run (an empty cabinet that fills), and History (a week of tape over a reading list).

2 · App detail and its cords

This follows Situation: Crafty in Trouble, Jellyfin waiting on nas when nas is offline, and Jellyfin with an update ready otherwise. The cord panel lists what the app needs and what needs it, and each row says where the cord came from. A suggested cord waits for your answer. Draw a cord opens a picker; the new cord is hollow, on the panel and on Home.

Where cords come from.

  • Found · mount: the app's container mounts a folder that is a nas share. Jam-Dash reads that from Docker.
  • Found · compose: depends_on or links in the app's compose file.
  • Found · proxy host: read from Proxy Manager, only after you connect it in Settings › Connections. Until then there are no proxy cords, and the note says so.
  • Yours: drawn by you here or on the rear panel. It's hollow and says when you drew it.
  • Suggested: two apps share a Docker network. That's a fact, but which one needs the other is a guess, so it's asked, never drawn.

5 · The parts

Annunciator

Five subjects × servers, in fixed places. Dark = fine, and the calm word stays readable in the recess. Lit red = down, amber + ▲ = needs a look, cream = waiting for you, dashed = not answering. Lit lights link to their cause; they never toggle. Holds 3 servers on desktop and 4 on the phone (rows). A fifth server folds calm servers into one row.

Rack, head unit, modules

A server is a rack. Its head unit carries three meters in a dark display window. Apps are 1U, or 2U with a live preview, in your order. Bare rails at the foot are room to grow and the visible end of the rack.

Running plate ★

When a rack is taller than the screen (Auto), healthy apps fold into one plate with a lit slot: "Running · 25 · Code Server, Crafty and 23 more". It opens into a shelf of half-width modules. Never folded: trouble, live previews, jobs, apps you keep in view.

Cords and the cord channel

Drawn only when you point at or focus a module; broken cords always. Solid = found, hollow = yours, dashed amber = broken. They run in combed lanes, at most 6 per module; the rest end on a plate with a count. A guess is asked, not drawn.

Maintenance tag

Trouble hangs on the tag rail: a coloured stub, a punched hole, light + label, title, one sentence, the fix. Fixed tags turn grey and say "Fixed 01:53".

Event tape

24 hours or the last hour, two lanes. Red = down, amber = warn, dashed = not answering, ticks = changes, a cream NOW rule. At 24 h, marks closer than 24 px merge into one mark with a count.

Bench

Updates, installs and first run happen on a bench with a walnut lip, beside the rack they change. Steps light slot windows, one orange per step, and the rack shows the same job.

Rear panel ★

Edit setup turns the module round: ports as jacks, folders, the environment as a terminal strip, secrets as dots with Replace, cords, power. Save says what it costs. Checks happen as you type.

Drive bays ★

Files' places are drive bays: a light, a name, the free space in a recess bar. A folder that apps mount names them (found).

Empty cabinet ★

First run starts with an empty rack. The apps found on this server rack in once (the signature move, under 600 ms in all). Adding a server brings a second empty rack that fills when it connects.
