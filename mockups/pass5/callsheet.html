Callsheet, pass 5: the whole app on one sheet

Pass 4 printed Today as a call sheet: a running order down a time column, a numbered list of who needs you, footnoted sources and a sign-off. Pass 5 keeps that and prints the rest of the app the same way: Setup becomes a form, Ask a transcript with sidenotes, People a matched pair, Projects a slip track, Know me a list of facts with their sources, and voice a reading rail. It also fixes the pass 4 audit. All names, mail, numbers and events are made-up sample data.

What changed since pass 4

  • Day windows read without the rim. On the light sheet an unlit window is a 1 px groove (an outline), and a lit window is flat cream in a 3 px dark bezel (a dark mass with light in it). The word beside it turns from dim to ink, and chosen options also press in. This is conflict 3's option B for light grounds, assumed pending Isaac.
  • The gap cue makes no time claim. "About 6 min" is gone (H17). A free stretch prints only the calendar's own fact ("Free until 11:00 · 55 min") and offers Work through them.
  • Said once, live wins. The summary is a snapshot. While a cue or a note on the same screen says the same thing, that sentence leaves the printed summary and its footnote number moves to the cue. Voice and the push still read the whole summary.
  • All five roster rows fit at 1440 × 900. The headline and summary share one line on desktop, and desktop roster rows take two lines instead of three.
  • No "Sent 0". The sign-off counts answered and for tomorrow, then says "Nothing was sent" in words. Its label no longer says "Wrap" (Isaac: "lets get rid of the cheeky production nods").
  • Undo happens where the change is. Done, later and moved rows stay as one struck-through line with Undo. Pass 4's toasts are gone (LY16). The dashed "Mockup" notes stand in for things that happen outside the app, like opening Gmail.
  • Redrawn: Setup (with a first run), Know me, People, Projects detail, Ask and voice. Phone overlays are now bottom sheets (CT12).

3 · New and redrawn elements

Day bezelDay windows on the light sheet: unlit is a groove in the paper, and lit is cream in a dark bezel. Seen at a glance or blurred, lit is a dark spot and unlit is nothing, so the state no longer depends on rim weight alone.
Struck lineDone, later or moved: the row stays where it was as one line, crossed through once (250 ms), with Undo beside it. Like crossing a name off a printed list. It goes when you do the next thing.
The form (Setup)Setup is printed as the form at the back of the sheet: lettered sections A–D, labelled fields, tick-box windows that apply at once, and no orange. Rare settings sit in closed drawers.
Call timesThe brief times are drawn as a short running order: time column, rail and a tick per sheet, with who writes it. Editing a time checks it as you type and says what it costs: "Careful: about 3–5 min, from the last 7 sheets".
Transcript with sidenotes (Ask)Chat is printed as a transcript, with no bubbles. Each question has a mono time tag, and each answer has numbered sources in the margin on desktop and under the answer on the phone. "How I found it" folds to one line.
Match gutter (People)Same person? sets two records side by side, field by field, with a gutter between them: = for a match, ≠ for a difference, · where one side has nothing. You can see why Callsheet asked. All three answers are neutral keys, so nothing pushes you to merge.
Fact list (Know me)What Callsheet believes about you, numbered, each with its source and Forget. Facts it wants checked are marked "To check". Checking them uses the same one-at-a-time pattern as replies.
Slip track (Projects)A project's weeks drawn as a strip with milestones. A slipped date keeps a dashed tick where it was, with an amber arrow to where it is now. Every date has a footnote to the mail it came from.
Reading rail (voice)Read it to me shows the sheet's own sections 01–04 down a rail, with a tick per spoken line and the lit slot on the line being read. On the phone the rail folds to a line strip, the way the running order folds to the day strip.
The blank sheet (first run)After setup, the sheet is shown empty: the four numbered sections as grooves, and "Your first sheet prints tomorrow at 06:30." One quiet key writes one now.
