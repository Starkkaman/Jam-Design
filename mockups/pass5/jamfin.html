JamFin, pass 5: the whole cinema

Pass 4 turned Home and the title pages into a cinema: a marquee names what you're on, profiles are tickets, a title's page is a printed programme, Play brings the house lights down, and a long pause becomes an intermission. Pass 5 fixes what pass 4 got wrong (row position now uses seat slots, the marquee waits 500 ms before it changes colour, the house lights make one counted change, the season keys are one press away, and long shows fit). It also carries the cinema into the screens pass 4 left alone: the library (a slim marquee and a letter rail), search (a letter board and a letter case), Settings › Appearance (the profile's own ticket), the player menus, and the phone (home, programme, player, the downloads reel, offline). All titles, people and art are made up.

Seat slots and a resting marqueePosition on the marquee is now a row of slots, one per card, with the one you're on lit cream (slot = "the one now"). Round windows only mean status again. The marquee's words follow focus at once, but its colour block waits for a 500 ms rest, so a held key never swaps the block. Try "Hold → for 2 s" on the films row.
One step of house lightsPlay dims the page in one fade (300 ms at night, 360 ms by day), then the title card slides in with the slatted bar showing the real load. By day the picture waits until a full second after the lights went down, so at most one big change lands in any second. Next episode skips the lights: the band loads it in place.
The rest of the buildingThe library gets a slim marquee and a letter rail. Search types onto a letter board from a letter case. Settings › Appearance edits the profile's own ticket, and changes show at once. Player menus rise above the band from the Options strip, five rows at most.

Phone (390 × 844, and the player at 844 × 390)

Each phone works: tap the tabs, cards, chips and keys. The same parts as the TV, by touch: tickets, the marquee, the programme, the episode list and the band. No focus windows, lifts or 500 ms rests, because nothing here follows a cursor. Five tabs, no "More", and one orange at most, which goes quiet while a sheet is open.
