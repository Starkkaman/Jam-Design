NervOS pass 5: the console, all the way through

The games launcher on the living-room TV. Pass 4 put the last game in the console and everything else on shelves seen by spine. Pass 5 keeps that and carries it into the screens pass 4 left alone: the Guide band is now the console's deck with the game standing in its slot, Power is the console's switch panel, Settings is its rear panel, the keyboard sits on the deck and writes on a label, a netplay room is the host's console with two ports, Signal lost is the computer popping back out of the slot, and Theater plays on the same deck. It also fixes pass 4's audit: names now show large and horizontally on every shelf (a name rail on the shelf edge), a Covers view for people who don't read spines, a lit shelf rail under the focused spine, ⏮ ⏭ on the remote to jump dividers, small sections stand face-out, and the attract board shows its place with slots. Click the TV and use the arrow keys, Enter (A), Esc (B), G (Guide), M (≡), Y (view), X, [ ] (L1/R1), , . (LT/RT or ⏮ ⏭) and 2 (a second controller presses A). Every game, film, computer, code and name is made up.

Names you can read from the sofa

Every shelf edge is a name rail: the focused thing's name in 46 px, horizontal, on the walnut. The spines keep their vertical titles for density. Y switches a shelf to Covers (face-out, about five per shelf) for anyone who finds spines slow. Sections with five things or fewer stand face-out on their own.

The console in every screen

Guide band = the deck, with the game in its slot and the players in its ports. Power = the switch panel. Settings = the rear panel with two-position slide switches. Keyboard = keys on the deck writing on a label. Netplay = the host's console, a free port is a free seat. Signal lost = the computer pops back out.

Shared on purpose

The Guide band and the player band keep the house key order, header and chooser rules (conflict 10, assumed B: the face is NervOS's). The launch keeps the house rule (opaque cover, slatted bar, max(load, 600 ms), B cancels, Calm fade) and draws its own cover (conflict 4, assumed B).
