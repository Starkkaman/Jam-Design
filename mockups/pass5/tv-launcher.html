Jam Design · TV launcher (Lobby) · Pass 5

Lobby: the lowboard under the window

Pass 4's living room, carried into every screen. The window's sky now follows the real sunrise and sunset from Home Assistant, and steps darker when the TV is in a bright picture mode. The app dial has three bands of six fixed slots, so it shows how many it holds. The lamps and the front door are drawn the way Jam Home draws them. An unlocked door is a warn by day and an alert after dark or when nobody's home. New this pass: All apps A to Z, Arrange the dial, Rename an input, Power and Restart the TV, and the idle face. Press Tour to watch it without a remote. All titles, art and names are made up.

Purpose first

Three people walk past this screen all day. It should be calm when nobody's using it, get anyone (a guest too) to their thing in a few presses, say what the TV is doing, and never sell anything. So it's furniture: a window, a sill, a lowboard.

New in pass 5

Three-band dial with fixed slots · All apps A to Z with index tabs · Arrange the dial · sun times from Home Assistant, with a day strip in Settings · darker sky for bright picture modes · Jam Home's dimmer plate and door swing · door warn by day, alert after dark · keyboard drawer to rename an input · Power and Restart the TV · the idle face.
