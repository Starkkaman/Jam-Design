How they differ

TelemetryFollows the device's theme. Grotesk words, mono numbers. Glow only in dark. Soft corners. The middle setting.
MissionAlways dark, even on the desk. Mono and condensed type, square corners, faint grid, stronger glow, a green clock. Loudest, most NASA.
BraunFollows the device, light first. One typeface, round corners, no glow or grid. Numbers in plain ink, so orange is the only colour you notice. Calmest.

These could be one default with two shelf variants, or settings for the volume dial: Braun on desk, Telemetry on touch, Mission on the TV. Tell me which you'd want.
