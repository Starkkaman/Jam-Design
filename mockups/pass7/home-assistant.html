Jam design · Round 7 · Home Assistant (Jam Theme + Jam Cards)

Jam Home, round 7: which switch is on

Round 6 with the switches changed and nothing else. Isaac found it hard to tell whether a switch was on, and the same paddle was on everything. Now lights keep the ON/OFF paddle, and on is a solid block (ink carrying the lamp's colour by day, the lamp's own colour by night); off is flat. Everything else that is plainly on or off (the Tree, the fan/blanket, the purifier and dehumidifier power, speaker grouping, automations) is a small switch: a dark channel that reveals an accent colour as the thumb slides across. The kiosks, remote, media cards, popups and purifier are otherwise round 6's. Everything is sample data (Tue 2:30 pm for Day, 9:40 pm for Night). Nothing here changes Home Assistant.

1 · Jam Theme

Callsheet's paper and navy, applied to the house

The values are Callsheet round 5's, with one step added for cards (a card sits a shade lighter than its ground by day and lighter navy at night). Orange, red, cream and walnut are the house constants.

2a · Switch accent

Which colour the channel reveals: three rules

The same six switches under each rule, all on but the last, Day and Night. The page uses A · House everywhere; the "Switch accent" control at the top applies B or C to the whole page instead. Every accent is fitted to the same lightness so the channel keeps ≥ 3:1 against both the off channel and the thumb. None may be the control orange, or read as warn, alert or ok.

6 · Language and rollout

What ties it to the other apps, and what a rollout would change

Shared with the family
  • Callsheet's paper/navy grounds, Jost for words, Plex Mono for labels and numbers
  • On / Off / Offline under every name; offline is a dashed card
  • One orange or none per screen: Lock (warn) > Pause > nothing
  • Red only for the door after dark or with nobody home, as a solid red key
  • Chosen keys press in (deeper paper + ink edge); nothing lights up to say "chosen"
  • The small channel-reveal switch for plain on/off (each app reveals its own accent; see the switch study)
  • Lights only where they're status: the door's round window, the purifier readout, lit power symbols, a playing grille's dot
Jam Home's own
  • The ON/OFF paddle, for lights only: on is a solid ON block in the lamp's colour
  • The house accent in the switch channel: Callsheet blue, the navy ink's hue lifted
  • Lamp symbols whose shade is the lamp's colour
  • The air purifier as a tiny appliance (the only seven-segment)
  • The remote (soft plastic, red IR at the top)
  • Source tiles with a printed colour band
Rollout (Jam Home's turn, with Isaac's OK)
  • Theme only: grounds, cards, hairlines, type (jam-theme tokens)
  • Card change: jam-light renders the paddle and the symbol; jam-tile and the popups render the small switch
  • Card change: jam-fan purifier restyle (no new card)
  • Kiosk: jam-kiosk's dock already has the shape; restyle only
  • No new cards. The plan, sideboard, key rail and Goodnight sheet from rounds 4–5 are dropped.
