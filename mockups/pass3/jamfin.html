What changed since pass 2

OK presses what is litOn the timeline OK pauses. Skip intro takes the light for 8 seconds when it appears (a slot drains on the key), so OK skips only while the prompt is visibly focused; then focus goes back to the timeline and ↑ reaches the prompt. Next episode never takes focus: it starts by itself, OK pauses and holds the countdown, ↑ then OK starts it now.
Seasons, and long seasonsMoving across season keys never loads anything until focus rests 500 ms (the house preview rule); ↓ or OK opens that season at once. A season with more than 12 episodes gets range keys (1–10 · 11–20 · 21–22) in the same row, so episode 21 is four presses away, not twenty.
Day, settledUnlit windows on light surfaces are a hairline groove instead of a dark dash; lit, they open to the full window with its dark rim. The title card takes the profile's lighting, so a Day Play makes one big luminance change (card to picture), not two.
Lower views filledBelow the actions the page works like Home: a sign (title, facts, two lines), the focused row, and the next row's name and cards: More like this then Cast (films), Seasons, episodes then Cast (shows), Chapters then Extras (discs).
Offline Home that does somethingTry again (orange), a key back to the TV's own Home, and a quiet automatic retry every 30 s. The last-synced Continue watching row stays visible, dimmed inside one dashed outline, so you can see what is waiting; it isn't focusable, because none of it can play.
