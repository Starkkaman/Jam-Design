Jam-Dash, pass 3: settled, and every screen it was missing

Jam-Dash (the Docker manager that replaces CasaOS, across two servers) in the simplified Braun Hi-Fi look. Pass 3 settles what pass 2 left open: the busy day, night bands, the warn mark, the alert key, long names. It also draws the screens that were missing: first run and adding a server, the full log, an app's setup (ports, folders, environment), and History. The apps, numbers, log lines, keys and files are made-up sample data. The host names are the README's "home-server" and "nas".

1 · What changed since pass 2

  • Busy day, decided (pass-2 audit #1, High). Home may scroll, but only below the faceplates. The hero, every trouble card, "To look at" and every server's faceplate are always on the first screen. A fifth ladder step, Rows, puts 40 apps on one 1440 × 900 screen. The same density can be chosen by hand (Settings › Home, per device). See section 3.
  • Night bands read as bands (#2). The band is a deeper black (#070706) with a rib every 5 px at 14% white, so the texture carries it, not only the edge. Pass 2 and pass 3 are side by side in section 4.
  • Warn folded into the LED (#7). The triangle window is gone. Warn is the round status LED lit amber, plus a printed ▲ in ink. That leaves four indicator shapes, as the bible has them.
  • The alert key (HA D84). An alert's fix is the solid red key with dark text. While it shows, nothing on the screen is orange. Only the first alert gets it; the other cards get plain keys.
  • One trouble-card shape (#6). The actions always sit under the text, at any count. It's the same grammar as Callsheet's Next card (D65). When only warns are left, the first warn becomes the card and takes the orange (#5).
  • Phone: the strip picks the server (#3). Tap a row and its square window lights; the grid below shows that server's apps. nas's apps now start where home-server's do, on the first screen.
  • Names wrap, never cut (#9). Tiles take two lines. Rows use a "Name on Home" you can set in Edit setup ("Nginx Proxy Manager" → "Proxy Manager").
  • Store heading tells the truth (#10): "Updates · 2 ready · 1 didn't take". New screens: first run and Add a server, Full log, Edit setup, History, Settings › Servers.

2 · Home, both servers

Switch Situation and Home density above. Click a tile for its detail, or its ↗ key to "open" the app. The loud button runs the fix. With an alert it's the red key, and nothing else is orange. After the fix it moves on: offline, then warns (orange), then nothing. History sits at the end of the hero's sub line. On the phone, tap a server in the strip to show its apps. On desktop, Enter presses the loud button, 1–6 switch places, and Esc goes back.

3 · The busy day, settled

These two frames always show the busy day (40 running apps, 3 alerts), whatever the Situation control says. The ladder now has five steps. Step 5, Rows, lines the faceplates up side by side under the trouble zone, then gives each server one-line rows: the name, the LED and word, and Open. At 1440 × 900 all 40 apps fit. At 1280 × 800 Home scrolls, but only below the faceplates, so both servers' state is still on the first screen.

The ladder, final
  1. Tiles drop their numbers line.
  2. Live previews become normal tiles with a short word.
  3. Stopped chips fold into "14 stopped".
  4. Faceplates go tight.
  5. Rows: faceplates side by side, apps as 36 px rows (5 columns at 1440, 4 at 1280; each row at least 250 px).
After step 5, the page scrolls. Never shrinks: the hero, trouble cards, "To look at", state words, Open keys, the disk meters.
The first-screen promiseHome may scroll, but nothing that says "is it OK?" is ever below the fold. That means the hero, every trouble card (up to 3, then "N more"), "To look at", and every server's faceplate. App tiles and rows may scroll. This replaces pass 1's "every app on one screen", which can't hold with 40 apps on a 1280 laptop.
Rows by handSettings › Home › Density: Auto (the ladder) or Rows (always). It's per device, like Look and Light/Dark. Someone who keeps 40 apps may want Rows on the laptop and Auto on the big screen.

4 · Settled parts: night band, warn mark, alert key

5 · App detail and the full log

Crafty is unhealthy. Restart is the red key, and Open, Update, Stop… and Remove… are plain. Full log opens the log full screen: follow or pause, All lines or Problems only, a time range, search (matches light cream), wrap, and Download. Press Edit setup for ports, folders and environment.

6 · Edit setup (ports, folders, environment)

One page per app with five cards: Name on Home, Ports, Folders, Environment and When it restarts. Each port and folder is checked as you type. Try typing 8080 as Crafty's web port: SABnzbd already uses it, so the check turns warn and Save waits. Secrets show as dots and are never shown back; Replace asks for a new one. Saving restarts the app, and the button says so. This screen is a form, so Save is its one orange, even while Crafty is alerting (a sheet or form is its own screen, D82).

7 · History

Everything that went wrong, and what fixed it, on both servers for 7 days. Open items keep a lit LED and say "Open now". Finished ones show an unlit LED and say how they ended ("Fixed 01:53 · restarted by you"), the same quiet form as Callsheet's seen alert (D69). Fix something on the Home frames above and it shows up here. There's no bell and no unread count: Home is the place for what's open now.

8 · First run, and adding a server

Four steps in step mode (D66): a slot window per step, one orange per step, and an end card. 1 · Set a password. 2 · This server: what Jam-Dash found, and the promise that CasaOS's apps keep running untouched. 3 · Add another server (optional): its address and a one-time code from that server's Settings. The first code you type is wrong, so you can see the failure. 4 · Done. The same Add a server sheet lives in Settings › Servers.

9 · Store and updates

Press Update all 3. Crafty and Sonarr update. Immich's new version fails its health check and is put back, and the heading changes to "Updates · 1 didn't take". Try again then takes the orange.

10 · State words (final)

The five shared hooks plus warn. Each state has three lengths: a lab word, a short word for tiles and rows (14 characters or fewer), and a sentence. "Fixed" and "Seen" are History's quiet forms of a finished alert or warn.
