What pass 3 settled

One way to switch inputs on HomeInputs are keys in the bottom row, beside the lamps and the front door (Roku tiles, webOS Home Hub). The band's input window is gone from Home. Over a picture, the TV menu's Inputs key is the other way in, and it's the only place that marks a current input.
"Current input", honestlyLobby and every app run on the TV's own source, called Apps. On Home that's what's showing, so no HDMI key presses in. The TV menu names what's underneath ("over Switch 2 · HDMI 2"), and its Inputs list marks Apps, HDMI 2 or Antenna with the square.
Quiet windows in DayAn unlit window is a shallow recess one step darker than its plinth. A lit one is cream inside a dark rim (D17). Twenty black dashes become one mark.
Banners and the accentReal banners sit in a 6 px card-coloured surround and are toned at night by a colour filter (Lobby floor 3). Stripes show only where there's no cover art: on the title card and the first-run Home.
Alerts and ends: one form eachThe front door's fix is a solid red key with dark text, the same as on the kiosk and phone (D84). Rows stop at their ends with a walnut mark, because Lobby's engine already stops there (D59).
New statesFirst run (an empty Continue with the one orange "Arrange Home"), an app that won't open (Try again + Home, the same ending as No signal), Lobby 1.4 ready (a cream light on Settings, never a pop-up), and Switch 2 turning on (asked once, in the clear zone).
