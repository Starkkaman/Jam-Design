What changed since pass 2

Slots live inside the bandSave, Load and Chapters swap the band's key row for a row of four picture cards. The band stays 230 px, so the paused picture is 79% visible instead of 49%.
The accent has a fixed placeColour bars sit right of the sign, below the band, in a strip that's never art or navigation. They show with Full colour covers too, one step quieter, and only step aside for a toast.
Steam keeps Guide? There's a way backIf Steam takes the Guide button, it opens Steam's own menu. Holding ⧉ + ≡ for a second (or Guide on the HA remote) opens the house band. The launch card and a first-time toast say so.
One TV grammar with the launcherThe band is the same 230 px. Row things light their bottom edge and column things their leading edge. Rows and the systems carousel stop at a walnut mark. A chooser's key shows ▲. Previews wait 500 ms. There's no veil, and hints follow the last input (A or OK).
The missing screensTheater player band, netplay room, Signal lost, Settings with choosers beside the list, Power (focus on Cancel; Restart and Shut down take a hold or two presses), the on-screen keyboard, a 1.6 s boot and the return.
Friendlier for guestsThe current section has a plain sub-label ("Archive · Retro games"). The game page says what Guide does. Errors say what happened and what to do next, in house words.
