Callsheet: fewer things, settled rules

Pass 3 settles pass 2's open findings and draws the screens that were missing: People, Know me, the full brief, the voice rundown ("Read it to me", for Wheatley in the car) and the offline states. It also counts the things on the first screen and cuts them. The look stays simplified Braun Hi-Fi with three shared parts adopted from the other apps: one orange per screen (D82), quiet rocker paddles (D83) and the red alert key (D84). All names, mail and events are made-up sample data.

What changed since pass 2

  • Alert and Priya together. An open alert keeps the Next card, but a Then line under it names the next reply and copies its draft (C). The alert's own key is the red alert key (D84), so a busy morning shows no orange at all. The "Alert card" control below switches to pass 2's strict card for comparison.
  • Counted once. Nav badges are gone. The rundown row shows the bar group and names the people after the one on the Next card; it never prints the count again. The bar group prints a number only above 10.
  • Warns get a time scope. A clash takes the Next card only within 60 minutes of its start. Try Time 10:05: the 11:00 clash takes the card, with the next reply on its Then line. Warns use the amber triangle window (D92).
  • Not for me is "Just this one" by default. "Everything from this sender" is a deliberate second key, and it's locked for people who always count. The rules it saves have their own list with Remove and Undo (Setup › Your day › Never needs a reply).
  • Setup has no orange. Rocker paddles are quiet (up + a cream window, D83), so Your day goes from four orange paddles to zero.
  • New screens. People (with the Same person? card), Know me (status, check what it wrote, questions), the full brief, Read it to me, and offline and error states (Google not answering; Callsheet unreachable; a refresh that fails).
  • Fewer things on the first screen. Every frame counts what it shows (the number on each caption). Measured by the same rule in both mockups (08:15, normal day): desktop Today goes from 44 to 36 things with the alert up and from 51 to 37 with a reply; phone Today from 35 to 30. The cuts: nav badges, the eyebrow, the band's summary and "now" label, labels for past and later events, the second tag, three of the Next card's quiet links (behind More), and on the phone the date, the band label and the Full brief link.
  • Calm has no orange (D95). See tomorrow and Back to Today are quiet keys. R plays the rundown (Space no longer clashes with focused buttons), B opens the full brief, and ? opens the key sheet.

1 · The flows, playable

Pick a flow, then step through it. Each step sets both frames, and both stay live. Desktop keys: Enter does the loud button, R plays the rundown, B opens the full brief, ←/→ step, 1–5 switch tabs, / or ⌘K opens Find or ask, C D L O N X work on a reply, J/K move through lists, ? shows the keys and Esc goes back. Click the desktop frame first so it takes the keys.

2 · The rules this pass settles

What gets the Next card1. An open security alert (red key, D84). 2. A source that's offline (dashed card, Try again). 3. A warn that starts within 60 minutes (a clash). 4. The next reply (Copy draft). 5. Calm: no orange at all. Anything that isn't a reply gets a Then line naming the next reply, with a quiet Copy draft.
Count onceA value appears once per screen. The rundown's bar group is the reply count on Today; tab badges are gone; step 2 says the number in words and links "See them all", not "All 7". The bar group prints its own number only above 10 windows.
Not for me, scopedThe default is Just this one. "Everything from this sender" is a second key, locked for people who always count. It never applies to invites or security heads-ups. Each rule shows when it was added and how much it caught, with Remove and Undo.
Content contract (from pass 2, kept)The Today sentence is 25 words or fewer and never repeats a time the scale shows. Rundown steps: one sentence, at most three items. Voice lines: one or two sentences each, the same facts as the step, no more.
VoiceRead it to me plays the same four steps. Captions show the current line only. Three big keys (Back, Pause, Next). It accepts "next", "back", "later", "repeat" and "stop", and nothing it hears can send, accept or delete. In quiet hours it opens with captions only.
OfflineA source that stops answering becomes a dashed Next card with one Try again, and its windows on the day scale go dashed. "Last read 06:30" appears once, on the card. If the box itself can't be reached, the phone keeps the last brief, Copy still works, and a dashed bar with Try again sits above everything.

4 · Stress and empty

These frames ignore the Day control. The long day has 19 replies, 7 events and 5 security heads-ups (the top of FLOW §5's range). The quiet day has nothing. "Large text" zooms the app body 120%, a stand-in for Easy read's bigger type, to check that nothing breaks.
