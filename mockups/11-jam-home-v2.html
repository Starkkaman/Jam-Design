Lighting

How the rules show up

Colour only for onRooms no longer get their own accent colour. A light's own colour shows in a small lit swatch window, never across the whole card. Orange is only on the switch paddles and the one primary button per card. Red appears in exactly one place: the unlocked door.
State words everywhereEach light says On, Off or Offline as well as showing a lamp. Offline is a broken form (a dashed outline) with no light in its window. The dock tiles say what's on ("2 on", "Playing") under their names.
One accent per screenThe iPad gets Stripes, the Echo gets Colour bars, and the phone gets Session blocks behind the room title. Each sits in its clear zone, never behind a control. Clock and room titles are paper-textured; small text stays solid.
