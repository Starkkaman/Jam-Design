Jam design · Pass 4 · Home Assistant (Jam Theme + Jam Cards)

Jam Home, pass 4: the house itself

Passes 1–3 converged on one template that every app now shares: cards on aluminium, rockers in the corner, a black ribbed band on top. Pass 4 keeps Jam Braun's calm and pass 3's decisions, but draws Jam Home as what it is: a house, used by three people, on phones and two wall kiosks. The home screens are a lit floor plan and a day arc. The light cards are wall plates you'd find beside a door. The media is a sideboard. Who's home is a row of keys on a hook. All of it is sample data (Tue 2:30 pm for Day, 9:40 pm for Night). Tap anything. Nothing here changes Home Assistant.

0 · What's new

Nine pieces that only make sense in a house

Kiosk + phone homeLit planThe bungalow as a flat plan. A room fills with the colour of its lamps while they're on, like a house seen at dusk. Tap a room to open it.
Kiosk + phone headerDay arcSun, time of day and weather as one calm line. The sun sits where it is; the forecast sits on the arc at the hour it arrives. At night it becomes the night's arc, with the moon and the kiosk's screen-off time.
Light cardDimmer plateA wall plate with a rocker paddle and a slide dimmer beside it. The lamp's own glyph is its indicator window: the shade lights in the lamp's colour.
Switched devicesOutlet plate · power stripThe Tree and the fan are smart plugs, so they're drawn as one: a plug module with a lit power symbol. The House page gathers every plug onto one strip.
MediaSideboardThe living room's TV, speaker and PC stand on one walnut-topped cabinet. Each object opens its own popup; the cabinet holds the one control for what's playing.
Who's homeKey railThree keys on a walnut rail by the door. Home = your key hangs on its hook. Away = an empty hook with a dashed outline. No map, no tracking dots.
LocksDoor swingThe architect's door symbol as the lock state. Locked: the door sits in its wall. Unlocked: it swings open in red, with the red Lock key.
NavigationRoom stripThe phone's tabs become a row of room windows. Each one is lit in its lamps' colour, so the tab bar is also the house at a glance.
EveningGoodnight roundOne sheet that walks the house before bed: what's still on, what's open, the door. It previews the dark plan, then does it in one press.
1 · Jam Theme

Tokens (short)

HA's own Jam Braun values (warm white, anthracite) instead of the aluminium the other apps share: HA is the app closest to what Isaac uses, so it keeps Braun's page and plate colours. The plan, door and key tokens are new and belong to Jam Home only.

2 · Jam Cards

The library, with every popup

Each card in Day and Night, live. Tap a name with › to open its popup (phone size here; kiosks get two-column versions, see section 3). Each card is drawn as if alone on its screen, so its orange is shown only where the card would earn it.

Real CSS sizes, scaled to fit. Day is Tue 2:30 pm (Amber out, the kitchen radio on, the window open). Night is 9:40 pm (everyone home, a film on the TV, Marissa's lamps on). 4 · Language check

What still makes it family

Same words, same windowsOn / Off / Offline / Locked on every plate, each beside a dark window that lights flat. Round LED for the door, square for a chosen scene, slot for running, bar group for a room's lamps.
Same type, same orangeJost for words, Plex Mono for small numbers. One orange per screen, and only when something is playing or it's bedtime; the solid red key fixes the door, and then nothing else is orange.
Same calmNo glow, grain, paper or brushed metal. Flat fills, a soft shadow on plates, walnut as trim. Seven-segment only on the purifier and dehumidifier.
What's Jam Home's ownThe plan, the arc, the plates, the sideboard, the key rail. No black ribbed band on the home screens (ribs survive only inside the TV's screen). No aluminium ground: Braun's warm white.
