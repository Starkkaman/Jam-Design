JamFin, pass 4: the cinema

JamFin's job is to help the household choose something to watch together and then get out of the way. The art is the star. Pass 4 drops the shared band-and-tiles template and borrows from a cinema instead: a marquee names whatever you are on, profiles are tickets, a title's page is a printed programme, Play brings the house lights down before a Bebop-style title card, and a long pause becomes an intermission. Pass 3's behaviour is kept: OK presses what is lit, the 500 ms rest, range keys, row ends, and Guide belongs to the launcher. All titles, people and art are made up.

Marquee (Home)A letter-rail board names the focused title. The title block takes the title's own colour, pulled dark so it can change on every move. The round windows on the right are the row's seats: one per card, the focused one lit. No "1 of 6" text. Session blocks sit in the marquee's right end and never touch art.
Programme (pages)A folded programme: title, facts and keys on the left page (the fixed zone), art, synopsis and credits with dotted leaders on the right. Shows get a running order instead of an episode row; the right page follows the focused episode after a 500 ms rest.
House lights, title card, intermissionPlay dims the page in two steps (the screen layer's alpha over black, not a rect), then a colour-block title card cuts in with the slatted bar showing the real load. A pause longer than a minute, or "Still watching?", becomes an intermission card over a colour-filtered picture.
