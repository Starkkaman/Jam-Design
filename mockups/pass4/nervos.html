NervOS pass 4: the shelf and the console

The games launcher on the living-room TV, designed for its own job: pick a game and play from the couch, for gamers and non-gamers, and get back into the last game fast. The game you played last stays in the console, so Continue is one press. Everything else stands on shelves you browse by spine, each system drawn as its own media. New this pass: the console, media spines with divider cards, the section slide, a player-select by controller port, the save strip, and an attract mode. Click the TV and use the arrow keys, Enter (A), Esc (B), G (Guide), M (≡), [ ] (L1/R1), , . (jump by divider) and 2 (a second controller presses A). Every game, film, computer and name is made up.

The console

Home is one object: a console with the last game still in its slot. The deck carries Continue (the one orange key), the walnut front carries the four controller ports. Launching drops the media into the slot; quitting pops it back up.

Shelves by spine

Each system is a shelf of its own media: grey cartridges, black clamshell cases, small handheld carts, jewel cases, arcade marquees, Steam big boxes, film cases. Divider cards with tabs (A–F, G–L…) are the range keys. A walnut bookend ends every shelf. About 15 games show at once instead of 5.

Moments

Pick up a controller (ports, not profiles: the first A is Player 1). The save strip (four frames on a film strip). Attract mode after 45 s idle: the shelf plays itself as a marquee, one change every 10 s, static in Calm, and nothing starts by itself.
