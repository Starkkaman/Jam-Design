Lobby: the console under the window

The living-room Hisense's home screen, redrawn for what it's for: a calm piece of furniture three people walk past all day. The top is a flat window whose sky follows the time of day. Continue stands on the sideboard as picture frames. The console face holds an app dial with a needle, a patch bay for the inputs, and a house plate with wall switches and the front door's thumb-turn. Starting something drops a slatted blind over the window. Same functions as pass 3 (no ads, no suggestions, one way in per job, OK on release). Press Tour to watch it without a remote. All titles, art and names are made up.

TOURSteps through 25 states on its own, about 4 seconds each. Click the TV or press a key to take over.

Purpose first

Three people walk past this screen all day. It should be calm to look at when nobody's using it, get anyone to their thing in a few presses, say what the TV is doing, and never sell anything. So it's furniture: a window, a sideboard, a receiver.

New elements

Sky window (time of day) · picture frames on the sideboard · app dial with a needle that remembers · patch bay with every port shown · wall-switch paddles · thumb-turn lock · TV readout key · drawer TV menu · slatted blind title card · a note in the window.
