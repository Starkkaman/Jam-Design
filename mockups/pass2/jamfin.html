What changed since pass 1

Title card holds only as long as the load600 ms floor so the shutter can finish, full form on the first Play of the session, a 300 ms short form after that. Flip "Title card hold" to Pass 1 and the status line shows the extra wait. At 13 s the card says so and Back stops it.
Said once, one ruleA card's bar is "how far". Its plinth says the runtime or "Watched", never "N min left". The sign and the pages say when it would end. The player's playhead carries its own time. An open layer carries its value and the thing under it stops saying it (a key's value hides while its chooser is open).
Stronger, quieter selectorThe window is 12 px tall in full cream; progress fills at 70% cream. The face lifts 14 px and brightens a step, the label snaps to cream and a baked spill fades in under the card. The orange key gets its own cue: it rises with a deeper under-edge and a lit window on a brown recess.
Sign in the band, 2.1 rowsTitle + one line in the black band (bible: one title + one line on home screens). Stripes sit in the band's own clear zone, under the tab line and right of the sign, away from the covers. They step back while the server is offline.
Menus pop above, below, or drop belowPlayer: chapters above the band, settings in the band (it lifts 74 px instead of 142), lists rise above their key. Pages: Audio and Subtitles drop below their key and cover the keys under it, so nothing pops beside.
