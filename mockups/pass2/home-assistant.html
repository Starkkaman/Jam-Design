What would change

Differences from Jam Braun today

Exactly what would move if this replaced the look you use now, with pass 2's picks. Everything else (page and card colours, soft corners, layout, the kiosk structure) stays as it is.

  1. Font: Archivo becomes Jost for words and IBM Plex Mono for small numbers. Both are OFL and would be self-hosted, like Archivo is today.
  2. Toggles become rockers: the olive pill toggle becomes an up-is-on rocker. When it's on, a small window on the paddle lights in the light's colour (cream for switches). The paddle itself is no longer orange (pass 1 made it orange). Olive leaves the palette.
  3. Lit lights: the whole-card tint (18 % / 24 %) becomes a tinted fader band at the bottom of the card (30 % / 24 %). The colour also shows in the slot window by the name, in the paddle window and in the fader. Off cards stay plain, as today.
  4. Numbers by sliders: on the phone, "75%" goes and the value rides the fader cap while you drag. On kiosks, the light faders become tall columns that print their number on the cap all the time.
  5. Chosen keys press in: the selected scene (ET66 yellow today) and the air purifier speed (orange today) become pressed-in keys with a lit square window.
  6. Much less orange: one orange button per screen, on what's playing (Pause on the TV). The speaker's Play, the NerveOS "⋯", the scene pill, the purifier speed and the grille dot are no longer orange. Today's Jam Braun Living view has six signal-orange marks (purifier speed, both Play keys, the NerveOS ⋯, the grille dot, the volume fill). Pass 2 has one.
  7. Room colours retired: every room heading gets the same walnut rule. Kiosk dock blocks lose their room tints. In Day they're card-coloured blocks with an indicator window; at night they sit on the black ribbed band.
  8. Black bands: the house strip and the TV screen become black ribbed bands, and so does the night kiosk dock.
  9. Kiosk clock: the colon goes from signal orange to the dim text colour.
  10. Unlocked door: the kiosk disc stays solid red (as today). The phone chip becomes a solid red "Lock" key (today it's a red dot and red text). The doors row's fix is the same red key.
  11. Air purifier: becomes the small appliance (grille, seven-segment PM2.5, round power key with a lit symbol). The house strip drops "Air · Poor" so the air reading isn't on the Living view twice.
  12. TV sources: still a 3-column tile grid. Every tile keeps a printed colour tab (like today's dots). The tile that's on now presses in and lights its slot (no yellow fill).
  13. Popups: they all share one sheet (Close top right, no orange Done). Light popups get five whites that show their colours. The speaker gets Group or split. NerveOS gets its own sheet. Offline lights get a small sheet with Try again.
  14. HA's own dialogs: switches and toggles in more-info turn ink and cream instead of the signal orange. The light slider keeps the light's colour.
  15. Kiosk Lights count: "4 on" becomes "3 on", because the Tree is a switch, not a light. It shows on its own row.
  16. Keys: pills become 12 px rounded keys; chips stay pills. Cards go from 14 px to 16 px corners.
