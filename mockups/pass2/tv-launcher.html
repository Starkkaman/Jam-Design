Lobby: the ad-free TV home, again

The living-room Hisense's home screen with no ads and no suggestions. It shows what you're watching, your apps in your order, your inputs, and the TV's own settings one press away. Pass 2 fixes the pass-1 audit: everything fits, the input on the TV gets its own window, progress and focus never share an edge, nothing is under 22 px. Click the TV and use the arrow keys, Enter (OK), Esc or Backspace (Back), H (Home), G or Q (Guide: the TV menu), M (≡ options), I (inputs), or the remote buttons. All titles, art and banners are made up.

What changed, and where it came from

Everything fits (pass-1 audit 1)Continue, Apps and Inputs all sit inside the 1080p safe area at once. The sign is one title, one line and the OK action on the same line, and app banners have a slim plinth.
The input gets a window (audit 8)The band holds a recessed source window, like a receiver's display: a lit slot and the input's name ("Switch 2 · HDMI 2"). OK drops the inputs list below the band. The Inputs row presses the current input in.
Progress and focus apart (audit 3, D13)Cards are art + plinth. Progress sits on the art's bottom edge, full width. The selector window is on the plinth's bottom edge, half width. The plinth says when it would end, not "N min left".
Guide opens one TV menu (D35, Google TV, Roku ✱, Fire TV)From Home, an app or an input, Guide raises the same band: Inputs, Picture, Sound, Sound out, Sleep, Front door, Settings. Choosers pop above their key (D16). The picture behind previews the mode as you move, 500 ms after you stop.
Order your own Home (Projectivy, FLauncher, Fire TV)All apps has two groups: On Home (in order) and Not on Home. Hold OK, or press ≡, for Open · Move · Remove from Home · App info. Move uses the arrows; OK drops it; Back puts it back.
Android settings, one entry (Projectivy, Google TV)Settings shows the values that matter inline (picture, sound, inputs, Home screen, sleep). Anything deeper goes to Android's own settings app, and Back returns to the same row.
