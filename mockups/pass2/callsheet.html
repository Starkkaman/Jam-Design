Callsheet: the rundown is the front door

Pass 2 starts from FLOW.md, the Callsheet session's answers about how Isaac actually uses the app. He opens it from the 06:30 push and reads it on his phone. He acts on three things: the summary, the next reply with its draft, and the day. He rarely opens Setup, but when he does he finds it the densest part. So every screen leads with one next thing, and the rest waits one tap behind it. All names, mail and events are made-up sample data.

What changed since pass 1

  • Wheatley rundown, step by step. The push opens a four-step rundown (your day, who needs an answer, worth knowing, coming up). On Today, the same four steps are a short list you can play again with Space.
  • The next thing works in place. The Next card is the next item: the alert to check, or the first reply with its draft, Copy and Done. FLOW §4 asks for this to be one tap from opening.
  • The moving primary follows D23. Alert, then a warn within the hour, then replies, then calm. The security sign-in now comes before replies. A page toggle shows pass 1's order for comparison.
  • Replies on desktop are list + detail again. FLOW says the detail panel and its copy button get the most use, and One at a time the least. One at a time stays one key away. Wrong items get a proper Not for me sheet that remembers the sender (Isaac's top Replies complaint).
  • Counts cap at 10 bars, with a full group plus the number after that (19). Clashes are warn (amber, ▲ and a word). An alert you've seen goes quiet ("Seen 08:20").
  • The accent steps back while anything needs you (D22). Stripes (darker than control orange) draw in once when the day goes calm. Colour bars and Session blocks are gone from Callsheet.
  • No hidden places (D26). Projects is a tab again, and Setup is a labelled header button. Setup is redesigned as four places of one-line rows, rare settings sit in closed drawers, and Start over is a dark button inside a confirm sheet (D24).
  • Jobs show progress in place (D29). Refresh and Ask fill a cream slot on the button that started them. Labels are 13 px (D30). Every pop-up is one sheet (D43).
  • Two lanes on the day scale, so a clash shows as two windows. All 18 invites are shown. "Now" fits on one line. J/K work in the list. A long-day stress case and a quiet-day empty state are included.
