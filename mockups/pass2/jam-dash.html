Jam Design · Overnight pass 2 · Jam-Dash

Jam-Dash, pass 2: fits, says it once, and both servers show on the phone

Jam-Dash (the Docker manager that replaces CasaOS) in the simplified Braun Hi-Fi look, reworked from the pass-1 audit. Home shows both servers. Trouble comes first and the one orange button sits on the next fix. A density ladder decides what shrinks when a screen is full. A new warn state covers "needs a look, not urgent". On the phone, a server strip puts both servers on the first screen. The apps, numbers, log lines and files are made-up sample data. The host names are the README's "home-server" and "nas".

1 · What changed since pass 1

  • Density ladder (pass-1 audit #1, High). When Home doesn't fit, it gives things up in a fixed order, and the frame captions say which step it reached. The hero, the trouble cards, state words and Open keys never shrink.
  • Warn state + per-app words (#2, High; D21). An amber triangle window means "needs a look, not urgent". Every state has a lab word, a short word that fits a tile (14 characters or fewer) and a sentence. See section 3.
  • Phone Home (#3, High). A ribbed server strip at the top shows both servers on the first screen and jumps to each one. Tiles are two-column name rows, and the numbers live in the detail.
  • Said once (#4). On the phone, an alert app leaves the grid while its card is up top. On desktop its tile stays in place as a marker (the word only, no reason, no buttons).
  • Warn has a place (#5): one quiet "To look at" line under the trouble cards. The faceplate shows 92%; the line says "310 GB left", so it's not the same value twice.
  • Orange order (#6; D23): alerts newest first, then offline servers, then warns. Try "Busy day": Ollama crashed last, so it gets the button. A calm Home has no orange at all.
  • Destructive (#7; D24): Stop, Remove and Delete are dark ink inside a sheet that names the consequence. Remove keeps the data unless you tick the box.
  • Night faceplates (#8): darker band #0b0b0a, a 1 px top highlight, a hairline edge and stronger ribs, so the "equipment front" survives at night.
  • Also: neutral line glyphs per kind of app instead of initials (D34). Sonarr's week uses small slots, not squares (D28). Tile numbers are 14 px. While a sheet is open, the page behind has no orange. Meters move only on a real change (Readings: Live). Offline Try again can fail. Files and Shares are one place, so there are 6 places on desktop and 5 tabs plus a gear on the phone.

2 · Home, both servers

Switch Situation above. Click a tile for its detail, or its ↗ key to "open" the app. The orange button runs the fix: the button fills a cream slot, the card blinks 3 times then holds, then leaves, and the orange moves to the next thing. When nothing is left, the Stripes accent draws in once. On desktop, Enter presses the orange button, 1–6 switch places, and Esc goes back. On the phone, tap a server in the strip to jump to it.

3 · State words and the density ladder

The five shared hooks stay (data-state on / off / offline / action, plus data-alert), and warn is added (D21). Each app picks its visible words. These are Jam-Dash's. Every short word fits the narrowest tile (phone, 175 px).

The density ladder (desktop Home)
  1. Tiles drop their numbers line (CPU · memory move to the detail).
  2. Live previews become normal tiles (they keep the slot light and a short word: "42 MB/s").
  3. The stopped chips fold into one link: "14 stopped".
  4. Faceplates go tight: no OS line, less padding.
Columns never grow: a tile stays at least about 190 px wide, so names and state words still fit. After step 4, the page scrolls. The hero, trouble cards, warn line, state words, Open keys and the disk meter never shrink.
Which thing gets the orange (D23)1. Alerts, newest first. 2. Offline servers. 3. Warns, newest first (an app's warn before a disk's). On a calm Home, nothing is orange: the routine action (Open) lives on every tile, so there's no single next thing. In the Store, Update all is the routine primary.
Meters on live dataReadouts update every 5 s as plain text. A bar moves only when its value changes by 3 points or more, with one 400 ms settle and no loop. A disk meter that crosses 90% turns amber in one step, and the "To look at" line appears. Calm mode: no movement, just the new length.

4 · App detail

Crafty, which is unhealthy. What's wrong, the fix, why (failing lines carry a ▲ as well as the colour), then resources, address and ports, and facts. Try Stop… and Remove… (both ask first, in dark ink), Update (it shows what's new first) and Problems only. From Home, open Immich to see a warn (one of its four parts stopped) or Jellyfin to see a healthy app, where Open is the orange button.

5 · Store and updates

The Store opens on updates, each with one line on what's new. Update all 3 is the orange button. In the run, Crafty and Sonarr update. Immich's new version fails its health check, so Jam-Dash puts 1.119.0 back, and the row turns warn ("It's running as before"). Try again then becomes the orange button. Get on a catalog app opens the install sheet, and the page behind it loses its orange.

6 · Files and Shares (one place, two tabs)

Places from both servers plus network shares, crumbs, the list, and a preview panel. Shares is Files' second tab on both sizes, which takes one item out of the top bar. Delete… asks first in dark ink. With nas offline, nas's places and the network share go dashed.

7 · Rules this mockup follows

Said onceThe reason and the fix live on the trouble card only. On desktop, the alert tile keeps its place with just the word, and on the phone it leaves the grid. The faceplate shows the disk percentage, and the warn line shows what's left. The hero counts only alerts and offline servers.
Orange, once, and only when there's a next thingThe first fix on Home, Restart on a broken app, Open on a healthy one, Update all in the Store, and Upload in Files. Behind a sheet, the page's orange turns grey. Stop, Remove and Delete are dark ink and always ask first.
Motion on change onlyMeters fill once on arrival and then move only on a real change. Cards slide into place when one leaves (FLIP, transform only). A job fills a cream slot in its button. New log lines rise in. While there's trouble, the accent steps back (D22), and it draws in once when everything is up. Calm cuts all of it to short fades.
