Design bible · Round 4 · Base look

Hi-Fi, flattened

A's colours, button, rocker switch and black continue band. C's calm numbered list and title card, now in a sans. "A touch + depth" is new: the list floats as a card, the black band sits on top of it, lamps are lit, and buttons have a bevel and press down. Ribs are spaced wider so they read as lines from a normal distance. The switch is now up for on, with the state written next to it. The progress bar is a lit strip under a shutter: press Play and watch the shutter slide back to reveal more light.

What carried over

From AAluminium, black and walnut, with orange for the one thing you press. The rocker switch, the orange button and the black continue band. The VU meter stays as a flat drawn dial.
From COne calm numbered list, three things at a time. The title card for Play and launches, now in Jost capitals with an orange stripe.
