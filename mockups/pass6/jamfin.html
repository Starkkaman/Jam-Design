JamFin, round 6: the dark room

Round 5's structure and behaviour, redrawn calm. The palette and type are Callsheet's dark navy. Each title stands on a few flat colour shapes. Play closes the split shutter over the screen, with the slatted bar as the load. There's no marquee, no tickets, no house lights and no lights on keys or rows. Focus is a cream ring and a small lift. The only light left is for a real state: the library not answering. All titles, people and art are made up. The art is placeholder colour fields.

Title shapes

Five compositions, each from two deep colours of the title's own art and one small bright shape. They stand on a hairline, like a set on a stage floor. The deep colours sit behind the type; the bright shape always sits clear of it. Each title keeps the same composition everywhere: Home, its programme, the shutter, the phone.

What's gone, what's kept

  • Gone: the marquee board and its letter rails, tickets, seat slots, house lights, the letter case, the intermission card, the reel, and every indicator window on keys, rows, tabs, choosers and the player.
  • Kept from round 5: the Home → OK → OK path to Resume, words that change at once while colour waits for a 500 ms rest, the season index one press to the left of the episode list, choices on the settings row, player lists rising above the band, the Options strip below it, and "Ends at".
  • Back: the split shutter as the Play transition. It's flat now: no ribs, grain, glow or brushed metal.
