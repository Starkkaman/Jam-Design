Jam-Dash, round 6: round 3's calm, with connections you can see

Jam-Dash runs Docker apps on two servers, "home-server" and "nas". This round starts again from round 3's layout (Isaac's favourite) and drops round 4 and 5's rack, cords and plates. It keeps one idea from round 5: point at an app (or Tab to it, or tap it on the phone) and the apps it needs and the apps that use it stay lit, everything else dims, and thin lines join them. A readout line at the bottom says what each link is and where Jam-Dash found it. All apps, numbers, addresses and log lines are made-up sample data.

1 · Home

Point at any app (try Sonarr, Proxy Manager, or one of nas's shares in its panel). On the phone, tap an app once to see its links and again to open it. In nas offline, press Show what waits on the nas card: the links that are broken right now are drawn dashed amber and stay up until you close them. Click a tile to open its detail; Esc goes back.

2 · How the links work

Drawn only when you askPoint, focus or tap draws them. At rest Home shows no lines at all, so "all good" still looks like nothing. Unrelated apps dim to about a third; the hero and the trouble cards dim a little; the server panels stay readable. Lines fade in once (220 ms; none in Calm) and go when you leave.
Three line forms Found = a Docker mount of a nas share, a compose depends_on, or a host in Proxy Manager (only once it's connected in Settings). A guess (two apps on one Docker network) is asked on the app's page, never drawn.
At scaleEach line ends on a small ring at the tile edge, and each linked tile gets a word: NEEDED or USES IT. Past 8 links the words drop and the readout counts. In Rows (the last density step) there are no lines: the highlight and the readout carry it. Links to stopped apps end on their chips; links to folded apps are only named in the readout.

3 · App detail

This follows Situation: Crafty in Trouble and Busy day, Jellyfin waiting on nas when nas is offline, and Sonarr when all is good. Each app's page has a Connections map: what it needs on the left, what uses it on the right, with the source under every name. Sonarr's map also shows a guess Jam-Dash asks about, and Add a connection, which draws one as yours (dashed).

4 · The density ladder on a busy day

This frame always shows the busy day (40 apps, 3 alerts) at 1280 × 800, whatever Situation says. The ladder is round 3's, cut from five steps to three: 1 Tiles with live previews · 2 Compact (previews become plain tiles, stopped apps fold into a count) · 3 Rows (one 37 px line per app, no link lines). Auto takes the first step that fits; if none fits, Home scrolls, but only below the server panels.
