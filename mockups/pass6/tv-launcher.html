Design bible · Round 6 · Lobby (TV launcher)

Lobby, cleaned up

A starting point, not a new concept. The room, the dial, the sockets, the lamps and the TV's sound and picture switches are gone from Home. What's left is what Lobby is for: carry on watching, open an app, switch to an input, and one quiet way into the TV's settings. The front door appears only as a line of text, with a Lock key when it's unlocked. Colours and type come from Callsheet's round 5 night sheet. There are two layouts of Home to compare: A Shelf puts everything on one page; B Panel shows one group at a time beside a short index. Press Tour to watch it without a remote. All titles and art are made up.

TOURTwenty steps, about four seconds each. Click the TV or press a key to take over.

The two layouts side by side

Both at rest, with the same content and the same settings as the TV above. They follow the controls (lighting, front door) so you can compare them in either state.

Removed

  • The sky window, sill, frames, lowboard, dial and needle, socket panel, wall plates, blind and drawer.
  • Lamps and every other Home Assistant control.
  • Picture mode, sound mode and sound out on Home (now in Settings).
  • Indicator lights in menus, status LEDs on keys, the update light.

Kept

  • Continue, Apps, Inputs, Settings.
  • The front door as one line: “Front door locked”. Unlocked, it turns amber and offers Lock. The TV doesn't unlock.
  • The split shutter as the launch card, flat, with the slatted bar.
  • Jost + IBM Plex Mono, cream for focus, one orange only on a failure screen.

Palette

  • Night: Callsheet's navy sheet #10141d, cards #1a2130, ink #ece5d3, dim #a29d8e, cream focus #f3e6c8.
  • Day (option): mid-light warm grey #cdc8bd with navy ink. Never paper-white.
