Indicator windows

Lights don't have to be green or round. There are four window shapes and five light colours, and an unlit window stays visible as a recess. Click any light to switch it.

Meters

Locked: the lit scale and ladder use one LED per notch, with a little bleed. Every notch is a window you can always see. Top row: on a card (follows your theme). Bottom row: on the black band.

Meters with readouts

The quiet face is back at its earlier readout size. Below it are three ways to give the lit scale its number. The fader drives everything here. Push it past 85 to see the red peak lamp.

Lit scale readout: seven-segment, two placements

A puts the bottom of the screen on the same line the half circle sat on. B centres it where the small circle was. In both, the unit is on a punch-label strip below the screen, not over it.
